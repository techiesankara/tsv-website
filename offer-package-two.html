Discover Penida Island

A three-night escape pairing your stay with guided visits to Kelingking Beach, Diamond Beach, and Angel's Billabong — the island's most photographed cliffs and lagoons.

Our team plans the day around tides and light, and a driver collects you from the resort, so the hill stays your quiet base between each outing.

Inclusions
  • 3 nights in a suite or pool villa
  • Daily breakfast at Puñña for two
  • Guided visits to Kelingking Beach, Diamond Beach and Angel's Billabong
  • Private car and driver for the island tour
  • Return harbor transfer on Nusa Penida
