Radha, the Beloved

Devotion, love, and spiritual connection, high above the island.

Radha (Sanskrit: राधा) is traditionally known as the beloved of Krishna, symbolizing devotion, love, and spiritual connection. The name represents a sanctuary of well-being where weary souls can pause, reconnect, and find a deeper sense of peace, high above the island and embraced by the serenity of Nusa Penida.

A tranquil wellness sanctuary offering holistic treatments inspired by Balinese healing traditions. Guided by experienced therapists, guests are invited to restore balance and relaxation in a serene hilltop setting.

Open daily 11.00 to 22.00
