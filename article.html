Getting to Nusa Penida from Sanur, step by step

Island Guide 12 September 2026 4 min read

Three legs, one afternoon, and someone from the resort at every hand-over. The crossing is the part of the trip guests ask about most, so here it is in order.

Nusa Penida sits across the Badung Strait from Bali, close enough to see from Sanur on a clear morning and far enough that the journey has three distinct parts. None of them is difficult. Share your flight details when you book and we arrange every leg; what follows is what to expect on the day.

1. From the airport to Sanur Harbor

A private car collects you from Ngurah Rai Airport, or from your hotel if you are already on Bali, and drives you to Sanur Harbor in East Denpasar. The drive takes around 30 minutes from the airport in normal traffic, longer in the late afternoon. The driver holds a sign with your name and knows which boat you are on.

If you land with time to spare, the harbor has cafés along the beach road. If you land close to the last crossing, tell us; we will book the boat with a margin and hold a later one in reserve.

2. The fast boat

The crossing takes 30 to 40 minutes and lands at Sampalan or Toyapakeh Harbor on the north coast of Nusa Penida. We handle the tickets and your luggage at both ends, so there is nothing to queue for at the counter. Seats are inside the cabin; the roof deck is open on calmer days.

Two things worth knowing. The sea is at its flattest in the morning, so an early boat is the more comfortable one if you have a choice. And the boat boards from the beach at Sanur: expect to walk a few metres through shallow water, and keep shoes that do not mind it within reach.

3. Up to the resort

Our driver meets you at the harbor. The resort is 5 km away in Ped, on the north coast, and the check-in desk is 15 to 20 minutes up the hill. The road climbs through the village and the last turn opens onto the strait, with Bali on the horizon behind you.

Door to door from the airport is about three hours, fast boat included.Distances from the resort, contact page

Timing the day

  • Morning flights into Bali connect to an early-afternoon boat and arrive at the resort in time for sunset at Sukha.
  • Evening flights are better paired with a night in Sanur or Denpasar and the first boat the next morning.
  • Check-in is from 14.00. If you arrive earlier, luggage is stored and the pool deck is open.

Going back

The return runs in reverse: the driver leaves the resort for the harbor with time to spare, the boat lands at Sanur, and a car takes you to the airport or your next hotel. For international departures, leave a full three hours between the resort and the airport, plus the airline's own check-in window.

Anything you are unsure about, the directions on the contact page list the distances, or ask the team on WhatsApp before you fly.
